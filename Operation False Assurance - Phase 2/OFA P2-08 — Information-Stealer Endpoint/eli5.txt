What we were looking for:
- Which address a password/data-stealing program (called an information-stealer, it's malware that grabs your saved passwords and files) sent stolen data to, and which web address it used.

What we found:
- The stealer sent data to 79.141.165.202, at the web address /a9b024dccb2b4f24.php.

How we found it:
- We searched the alarm log for a known password-stealing program called "StealC" and found matching warnings from the sick computer.
- Those warnings pointed to one specific address.
- We looked at the actual web message and saw it was a POST (a way of sending data) to a specific page, matching the alarms exactly.

Answer:
CTK{79.141.165.202|/a9b024dccb2b4f24.php}
