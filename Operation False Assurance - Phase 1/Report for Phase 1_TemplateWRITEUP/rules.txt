📢 IMPORTANT ANNOUNCEMENT — NO EVIDENCE, NO POINTS
As advised by our Team Leader, the “NO EVIDENCE, NO POINTS” policy will be strictly enforced.

All findings and answers included in the investigation report must be supported by clear and verifiable screenshots taken from the actual investigation environment.

The screenshot requirement will serve as one of our primary measures to counter unsupported AI-generated answers. Teams may use AI as a research or investigation aid, but simply submitting an AI-generated answer or explanation will not be accepted.

For all Report-Required categories:

No screenshot evidence = No points
Screenshots must come from the actual tools or platforms used during the investigation, such as Security Onion, Elastic/Kibana, Wireshark, terminal output, logs, or other relevant forensic tools.
Screenshots must clearly show the evidence supporting the team's answer or conclusion.
Screenshots that only show the final answer, AI-generated response, or manually typed information will not be considered valid evidence.
Evidence must demonstrate that the team actually performed the investigation and validated the finding.
A report with missing, insufficient, or unverifiable screenshot evidence will NOT receive a PASS evaluation.
The corresponding flag will NOT be released until the report satisfies the evidence requirements and receives a PASS evaluation.

Teams are strongly advised to take screenshots while conducting the investigation and ensure that important queries, logs, timestamps, indicators, packet details, and other relevant information are visible.

AI CAN HELP YOU FIND THE ANSWER — YOUR SCREENSHOTS MUST PROVE IT.

NO EVIDENCE. NO POINTS. NO PASS. NO FLAG.
